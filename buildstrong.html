BuildStrong

The business runs. It runs through you.

You built it, and every hard call still stops at your desk. The price rise you have been putting off, the hire you are not sure about, the client who is a third of the turnover. BuildStrong is six months of working those decisions through, one every fortnight, at a whiteboard, with somebody who has no stake in the answer.

01 · The Problem

You have written the processes down. They still ring you.

Everything that still reaches you is a judgement call. The client threatening to leave. The two bad options. The quote that does not fit the price list. None of it can be documented, because it was never the same situation twice.

So you decide alone, usually in the van or at eleven at night, and then you lie awake marking your own homework. Your team cannot tell you when you are wrong. Your accountant tells you how the year went months after it ended. Your family wants you off the phone. There is nobody in your week whose job is to think about the business with you, and it shows in the decisions that keep getting pushed to next month.

That is what BuildStrong is for. Not the work. The deciding.

03 · The Work

Six months, with an end you can see from the start

BuildStrong is not a retainer and it does not run on. It is six months with two or three outcomes we agree before it begins, a midpoint at three months where we check them, and an end date after which you carry on the thinking without me.

i.

At the start.

In the first session we agree what has to be different by the end, in two or three figures you already keep: prices up and the margin that follows, a client won without you in the room, a fortnight away without the phone going. They go on the whiteboard and they stay there for the six months.

ii.

At three months.

We hold the outcomes up against where the business is. If the work is doing what it should, we carry on. If it is not, you can stop there and pay for the half you have had. You run a business; you would not sign anything without a break clause, and nor should you here.

iii.

At six.

The outcomes are met or they are not, and we say which. By then you have done this often enough to run it yourself: the question, the board, the decision. Some owners come back for a second six months on new outcomes. Nobody drifts on.

Six months, a session every fortnight, so twelve at the board. £3,600 for the whole engagement, which is £300 a session, with the option to stop at three months and pay £1,800. No retainer, and nothing added on.

04 · A Session

You bring the decision. We leave with it made.

Every session is a Whiteboard Session. Your thinking, out of your head, onto the board, into a solution. What goes up is your call, and it is always something live in the business this week.

You bring the live one.

The thing you have been going round in the car. Not a reflection on last year. The decision that is costing you money or sleep right now.

We work it on the board.

I write as you talk, so the whole of it is where you can see it. Then we find what connects, what is missing, where your own way of running the place is sitting inside the problem, and what will actually hold. Nothing between sessions. The thinking happens here.

You leave with a decision, or the action that gets you to one.

Written on the board before we finish, and the first thing I ask about next time. And I say what I think. You are not paying for somebody to nod. When I have a view you get it, marked as mine, and you do what you like with it.

Online with the board shared live on screen, or in person in central London or Milton Keynes. Nobody else is in it. You pay, so there is no sponsor, no report and nobody to answer to but you.

Six months is long enough to get through most of this list. You choose the order.

07 · The add-on: coaching your second in command

When the decision is to hand over, there is a second piece of work

Sometimes what comes off the board is that somebody else has to be able to make the calls. Not the processes, which you have written down. The judgement. That is built in a person, by exposure and correction, over time, and it is a separate piece of work with its own price.

You have probably tried to hire it.

Different names, different years, the same role. Every one of them was fine at week six. Something happens around month three, when a senior person stops learning how things are done here and starts deciding things. Hiring is a way of buying somebody who already knows how you decide, and that is not on a CV.

Being good at it makes you worse at teaching it.

When you have done a thing ten thousand times you stop seeing the steps, you see the answer. So watching somebody work it out slowly is hard, and you step in, every time with good intentions. Developing people is a specialism nobody taught you. That is a gap rather than a flaw, and gaps close.

What the add-on is.

Six sessions over three months with the person who could run things when you are not there. Live decisions worked through at the board, scenario practice off stage for performance on stage, and the work of merging their own values with the role so that what comes out is theirs rather than an imitation of you. You and I check in throughout, because the hardest part of this is usually not their development.

£1,800 for the six sessions, which is £300 a session. Open to BuildStrong clients and to at.theboard members, at the same price.

The aim is not that you step back entirely. Most owners do not want that. It is that the business runs for a fortnight without you and nothing needs deciding twice.

08 · When this is not the answer

Sometimes it is process.

If nothing has ever been written down and you are answering the same question forty times a week, an hour at a whiteboard will not fix that, and I will tell you so and send you off to write it down. If the decisions that reach you are repeatable with clear rules, that should be a system rather than your judgement. And if what you need is five other owners rather than one coach, at.theboard is the room for that, and I will say so.

I would rather say any of this in the first hour than three months in. It costs me the work, which is fine.

09 · One thing worth knowing

The point is to leave you able to do this without me.

Not new frameworks and not a programme. The deciding you would already be doing if your week had an hour in it with nobody wanting anything from you, done often enough at the board that by month six you run it yourself. If you come back, it is for a new set of outcomes, not because the first set never closed.

A feature for The Leader and the Team

TeamStrong.

For the leader who has changed how they lead, and now needs the team to come with them.

01 · The Problem

You have moved. The team is still where you left it.

Something has shifted in how you run things. Maybe it came out of the one-to-one work: a new direction you are now sure of, a standard you are no longer willing to drop, a decision that the business cannot keep routing through you. You can see it clearly. They cannot, yet.

So the meetings still go the way they used to. The team still waits for you to make the move you wish they would make themselves. The new direction is on a slide somewhere, and the old habits are in the room. Good people, working hard, and still thinking around you rather than with you.

That is not a people problem. It is the normal gap between a leader who has changed and a team who have not been given the means to.

TeamStrong is six months of closing that gap, with you and with them.

02 · Where it starts

Four reasons a leader brings the team in

Most TeamStrong work begins at the end of a LeadStrong or BuildStrong session, when the decision on the board turns out to need more than one person to carry it.

A new direction.

You have decided where the next two years go, and you can say it in a sentence. The team heard the sentence and went back to what they were doing. Alignment is not an announcement. It is six months of the team making decisions that fit the direction without checking with you first.

A new culture.

The way things get done here has to change: how people disagree, how work gets handed over, what happens when something goes wrong. You cannot instruct a culture. You can build one, deliberately, with the people who will live in it.

New standards.

You have raised the bar on something, quality, pace, how the client is treated, and the team is finding out by getting it wrong. The standard needs to be understood, not just stated, and the team needs to hold each other to it rather than wait for you to.

More carried by the team, as you step up.

You are moving into the strategic work, the part only you can do, and that means the team has to run the rest without you in every decision. Not because you have gone, but because you are finally doing your job.

If one of these is where you are, the team version of the work is the next step, and it starts with you.

04 · The Work

Six months, in three strands, ending with a team that runs itself

TeamStrong runs alongside the thing you are trying to change, not instead of it. The direction, the standard or the handover is the live material; the work is the team learning to carry it without you in every room.

i.

You.

Your own LeadStrong or BuildStrong sessions carry on alongside, so what you want from the team and what you are seeing in them is worked at the board before it is worked in the room. You stay the leader of this. I am the person who makes it possible for you to lead it.

ii.

Each of them.

Every member of the team completes CliftonStrengths 34 and from it their FITT16 profile, then has a one-to-one with me to understand it. Most people have never had an hour on how they are actually wired and what that does to the people around them. It changes how they turn up to the next team session.

iii.

All of you.

Monthly team sessions at the whiteboard, on the real work: the decisions in front of the team this month, worked through together until they are made. Not team-building. The team, building the thing you have asked for, and learning how it decides while it does so.

Month 1

Your outcomes agreed; everyone's CliftonStrengths and FITT16 completed.

Month 2

Individual one-to-ones; first team session.

Months 3 to 5

Monthly team sessions on live decisions, the team taking more of the room each time.

Month 6

The team runs a session with me watching rather than leading, and we judge the outcomes together.

Teams that want a day away from the building can add the Experience Day at The O2: a morning workshop, lunch at TOCA Social, an afternoon at Activate. It is a good day and it is optional, priced separately and at cost. The work does not depend on it.

One price for the six months of team work, sized to the team and agreed before we start. Teams of four to twelve. Your own one-to-one sessions are a separate engagement and run alongside.

05 · How the team learns itself

Sixteen ways of being driven, and your team has five or six of them in the room

Most teams know what each other does. Almost none know what each other is for: what drives a person, what they will go to the wall for, and what they quietly cannot stand. That is what FITT16 shows, and it is why it carries the understanding work in TeamStrong.

i.

FITT16, the main lens.

A profile of sixteen types, built from each person's CliftonStrengths Full 34, that maps the drive underneath their strengths. Not what they are good at, but what they are reaching for. When a team sees its FITT16 types side by side, the arguments that have been going on for years make sudden sense: two people pulling in different directions because they are driven by different things, not because one of them is difficult. The team's collective drive shows up too, and so do the gaps in it.

ii.

CliftonStrengths, underneath.

The 34 themes are the raw material the FITT16 profile is built from, and they stay useful at the practical level: who should run which meeting, who checks the detail, who is wasted on the detail, how two people with opposite profiles hand work to each other without friction. The team learns to read each other's profiles and uses the language day to day.

iii.

Why it matters for productivity.

A team that understands why its members behave as they do stops explaining things three times and stops routing round each other. Work gets handed to the person built for it. Disagreements get shorter because everybody knows where they are coming from. The leader stops being the translator.

Every member gets their own profiles and an hour with me on them. The team gets the map of all of them together, on the wall, for the six months.

07 · What it leads to

A team that decides without you, in the direction you set

By month six the team runs a session while I watch. They bring the decision, they work it, they make it, and the question "what would John want" does not come up because they already know, and because they have stopped needing to ask. For an owner, that is the first time the business has run on something other than you. For a corporate leader, it is the team your next role needs you to have already built.

08 · When this is not the answer

Sometimes the team is fine

If the team is doing what you asked and the problem is that you have not yet decided what to ask, that is one-to-one work, and TeamStrong will not help until it is done. If the real issue is one person who should not be there, six months with the whole team will not fix it and I will say so. If the team is bigger than twelve, it is two teams, and we talk about which one goes first. And if what you want is a good day out for a team that is already working, book the O2 day on its own and skip the rest.

I would rather say any of that in the first hour than three months in.

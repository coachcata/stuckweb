One reader. Six moments.

Your control panel for choosing which link to send. This page is linked from nowhere. Keep it that way.

  • · The front door The public index: five doors, no descriptions. Safe to post anywhere; this is the link for profiles and bios. →
  • i. Fine. Just busy. For the leader who would say everything is fine, and mean it, and be almost right. Unaware and satisfied; send only via a trusted peer or after a conversation that earned it. →
  • ii. So you've decided this is what the job costs. For the one who knows something is off and has filed it under the price of the role. Aware, not looking. →
  • iii. Something has put it in front of you. For the week it stops being theoretical: a new remit, an inherited team, a decision that will not land, a role ending. →
  • iv. You're weighing ways to get help. For the one genuinely comparing options. The only page that speaks directly. →
  • v. Good. Let's get an hour in the diary. For the one who has decided, and just needs the diary to cooperate. →
  • vi. You did the work. It's still working. SEND-ONLY. For clients present and past: the open door, the letter, the referral line. Never post this publicly and never list it on the front door; it is earned, and it is sent person to person. →
  • · An Hour at the Board, On Your Own The gift. Ungated, whole, safe to send to anyone at any point. The early pages all point here. →

Rules of the road: one link per message, ever. The link follows recognition, never opens with it. Nothing goes to anyone whose situation you are guessing at. Onward is earned. This map is yours alone.

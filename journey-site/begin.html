For when you've decided

Good. Let's get an hour in the diary.

The deciding was the hard part, and it's done. What remains is admin, and there is very little of it.

Choose a time

Pick whatever suits. A confirmation and calendar invitation follow automatically, and if the week does what weeks do at your level, moving it is no trouble.

What the first session is like.

You arrive, in person or on screen, and the board is empty. You start with whatever is actually live for you, and you think it through together, out loud, until it is clear. John is the other mind in the room: asking the question you had stopped asking, catching the thing you have gone blind to because you see it every day. You leave with a real conclusion. The question answered, the next move clear, or the decision made.

What to bring.

Whatever is actually live for you. The decision that will not land, the team dynamic that is stuck, the question about next year you keep not getting to. It does not need to be tidy; half-formed is what boards are for. If several things qualify, bring them all. The first ten minutes usually sorts out which one needs the hour.

There are no forms beforehand, no deck to prepare, and no pre-reading. The preparation is deciding to come, and you have done that.

If you're joining online.

Online sessions run identically. The board is shared live on screen, the thinking takes shape in front of you the same way it would in the room, and the session ends the same way: when the thing is clear, not when the clock says so.

Anything else

If a question is sitting between you and the booking, write to coach@catalystgrowthcoach.co.uk. John reads everything that comes in and replies within one working day.

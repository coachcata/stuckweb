WHAT THIS IS

A standalone site, completely separate from the live site. It contains
five public pages for five different weeks, a public front door, one
gift page, one client-only page, and your private routing map.

  index.html                the PUBLIC FRONT DOOR: five doors, one gift
                            line. Safe to post anywhere; this is the
                            link for profiles and bios.
  fine.html                 "Fine. Just busy."
  the-cost.html             "So you've decided this is what the job costs."
  this-week.html            "Something has put it in front of you."
  the-options.html          "You're weighing ways to get help."
  begin.html                "Good. Let's get an hour in the diary."
  onward.html               For clients past and present. SEND-ONLY:
                            never posted publicly, not listed on the
                            front door.
  an-hour-at-the-board.html The full written piece, free and ungated,
                            with a print option.
  map.html                  YOUR PRIVATE ROUTING MAP. Linked from
                            nowhere. Bookmark it; never share the
                            address.

Pages one to four each end with a single quiet line that opens the next
door, so a reader who wanders in at the wrong page can find their own
way. Nothing anywhere names the architecture.

HOW TO PUBLISH IT (about five minutes, nothing technical)

  1. Unzip the file you were sent. You will get a folder called
     journey-site with index.html inside it.
  2. In your web browser, go to:  https://app.netlify.com/drop
  3. Log in if it asks (the same Netlify account the main site uses is
     fine, or a free new one).
  4. Drag the whole journey-site FOLDER (not the zip) from your computer
     onto the big drop area on that page.
  5. Wait for the upload bar to finish. Netlify then shows you a link
     that looks like  https://something-random.netlify.app
  6. That link is the front door. The other pages sit behind it, for
     example  https://something-random.netlify.app/fine.html  and your
     private map at  .../map.html
  7. Optional: in Netlify under Site settings you can rename
     "something-random" to a name you prefer, or attach a domain later.

STILL WORTH KNOWING

  1. Booking link. All booking links use the live site's scheduler (the
     address ending ...aba1a55/schedule), which is what the contact page
     of catalystgrowthcoach.co.uk uses today. Your original brief named
     a different scheduler address; if that one was deliberate, say so
     and it can be swapped in four files.
  2. The pages carry a "noindex" tag, so search engines will not list
     them. Readers arrive only by the links you send or post. If you
     ever want search engines to find the front door or the gift page,
     the robots line near the top of those files comes out.
  3. The testimonials on the-options.html are word-for-word from the
     live site except one dash became a comma (site style uses no
     dashes). The Ralph Shorthose quote was left out because it
     contains a word these pages avoid.
  4. One deliberate judgement call: the written piece contains the
     sentence "You will know this stage is done when you slow down."
     The word appears nowhere else on any public page, and here it
     plainly means a step of the exercise, so the piece was kept
     verbatim as instructed. If you would rather it read "this part is
     done", change that one word.
  5. Fonts, images and videos are all inside the folder; the pages
     depend on nothing external except the LinkedIn and booking links.

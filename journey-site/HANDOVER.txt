CATALYST GROWTH COACHING, THE JOURNEY
Handover note for John
=====================================

WHAT THIS IS

A standalone six-page site, one page per stage of the considered-purchase
journey, plus a routing index and the free-tool page. It is completely
separate from the live site and does not touch it. Open index.html to see
the map of all six pages with one line on who each is for.

The pages and their files:

  index.html                index for you, links all six with routing notes
  fine.html                 Stage 1, unaware and satisfied
  the-cost.html             Stage 2, aware but not looking
  this-week.html            Stage 3, the breaking point
  the-options.html          Stage 4, assessing options
  begin.html                Stage 5, purchase
  onward.html               Stage 6, review, clients past and present
  an-hour-at-the-board.html the free self-guided thinking hour

HOW TO PUBLISH IT (about five minutes, nothing technical)

  1. Unzip the file you were sent. You will get a folder called
     journey-site with index.html inside it.
  2. In your web browser, go to:  https://app.netlify.com/drop
  3. Log in if it asks (the same Netlify account the main site uses is
     fine, or a free new one).
  4. Drag the whole journey-site FOLDER (not the zip) from your computer
     onto the big drop area on that page.
  5. Wait for the upload bar to finish. Netlify then shows you a link
     that looks like  https://something-random.netlify.app
  6. That link is the live site. Open it, check it, and share the
     individual page addresses whenever you want to route someone, for
     example  https://something-random.netlify.app/fine.html
  7. Optional: in Netlify under Site settings you can rename
     "something-random" to a name you prefer, or attach a domain later.

The email forms (the newsletter and the free hour) work automatically
once the site is on Netlify. Submitted addresses appear in the Netlify
dashboard under Forms: one form named "considered-leader" and one named
"hour-at-the-board". No setup is needed, but do submit a test yourself
after publishing to see them arrive.

SMALL EDITORIAL NOTES

  - The three testimonials on the-options.html are the real ones from
    the live site (Mark Schmull, Martin Malins, Stuart Ploughman),
    unchanged except punctuation: the site style here uses no dashes,
    so one dash inside the Schmull quote became a comma. No words were
    changed. The Ralph Shorthose quote was left out because it contains
    a word this set of pages avoids.
  - All photos, videos and client logos are reused from the existing
    site. Fonts are included in the folder itself, so the pages depend
    on nothing external.
  - The pages carry a "noindex" tag, meaning search engines will not
    list them. That is deliberate while the placeholders above stand,
    and because you route people to these pages directly. If you later
    want them found by search, remove the robots meta line near the top
    of each file.

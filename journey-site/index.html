The journey

One reader. Six moments.

Each page is written for exactly one state of mind, and written to be sent alone. This index is for choosing the right one.

  • i. Fine. Just busy. For the leader who would say everything is fine, and mean it, and be almost right. →
  • ii. So you've decided this is what the job costs. For the one who knows something is off and has filed it under the price of the role. →
  • iii. Something has put it in front of you. For the week it stops being theoretical: a new remit, an inherited team, a decision that will not land, a role ending. →
  • iv. You're weighing ways to get help. For the one genuinely comparing options. The only page that speaks directly. →
  • v. Good. Let's get an hour in the diary. For the one who has decided, and just needs the diary to cooperate. →
  • vi. You did the work. It's still working. For clients present and past: the open door, the letter, and a word about introductions. →

The free tool the early pages point to

An Hour at the Board, On Your Own

A self-guided thinking hour. Pages one to three offer it before anything else is ever mentioned.

A note on the design: nothing on pages one to three sells, names a service, or asks for anything beyond an email address, and that is deliberate. Recognition first; help freely; the direct conversation only when the reader is already comparing options.

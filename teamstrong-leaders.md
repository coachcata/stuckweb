# “We spend the meeting on the numbers. The real story is in how we lead.”

For leadership teams of five to ten where each leader runs their own region, function or division. You are not trying to become one team. Each of you is growing in how you lead your own people, and you are doing it alongside each other.

## Everyone reports on their team. Nobody reports on themselves.

### If you sit around the table

> “I know exactly how my numbers are doing. I am less sure how I am doing.”

> “My team takes its lead from me. I am not always sure what it is taking.”

> “I can see what is not working in my area. I am less sure how much of it starts with me.”

### If you lead the leaders, or look after them

> “Some of my leaders are further along than others. We all know it, and nobody says it.”

> “I want every one of my leaders to grow, each at their own level. This is how I invest in all of them at once, and nobody is singled out.”

## A clearer view of how each of you leads

It is hard to see how you lead from inside your own area. So we step outside it together and look from a different angle, one that shows each of you how you come across to the people who report to you. Each leader leaves knowing what they want to change.

## One day, and every leader takes something back to their team

A single, high impact day at your venue or ours at the Shire Retreat, Milton Keynes, in the setting you choose. Each leader leaves with a clear picture of how they show up, and two or three changes to take back to their own team.

### The immersive building experience.

A team building experience that every leader takes part in. On a mini construction site with real small diggers and machines, six metres by three and brought to your venue, you plan and build together through competitions and activities. Each leader’s habits show up quickly, and we talk about how you communicate, how you plan, who takes ownership and how you work with others.

Site footage coming soon.

### The board.

Each leader brings a live problem from their own area, and we harness the creativity and problem solving of the whole group, live at the whiteboard. Problems that have been running for a while get solved, and I guide each leader to set out a plan to put the solution into action and test it.

### The strengths lens.

When cohesion and collaboration are the focus, you need to go beyond skin deep. Everyone completes FITT16 and CliftonStrengths beforehand, so each leader understands the others at a deep level, and sees what their own way of leading means for the people who report to them.

From £3,500 plus VAT, depending on the size of your team. One price for your team, agreed up front, with everything included.

## Each leader growing, month by month

One high impact session a month, each in a different setting, so the growth keeps moving and has time to show up in each leader’s own team. The mix is chosen for your leaders. A typical shape:

- Month one, the immersive building experience.How each of you leads today.
- Month two, the strengths lens.Why you lead that way.
- Months three to five, the board.Real challenges from each area, with actions and dates checked each month.
- Month six, back to the immersive building experience.The same task, and a clear view of how each of you has changed.

Leaders can have their own one-to-one sessions alongside where it helps. Price agreed with each team.

## Questions

### What space does the immersive building experience need?

Two areas side by side: one for the immersive building experience, which is six metres by three, and one for horseshoe seating where we talk. It works indoors or outdoors, and the machines are self-powered, so no power is needed.

### What if someone would rather not drive the machines?

That is fine. There is plenty to do without driving, and planning the task is often where the most revealing conversations happen.

### What is included in the price?

Everything. The One Day Programme is one price for the whole package, immersive building experience included, from £3,500 plus VAT depending on numbers. For the Six Month Programme, we agree one price before we start, and it includes the one-to-one sessions.

### Our leaders do not work together day to day. Will this still work?

Yes, that is exactly who this page is for. The aim is not to make you one team. It is for each leader to see how they lead their own people, with the others alongside.

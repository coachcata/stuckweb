## Out of your head and onto the whiteboard

Each session runs for 90 minutes.

### Everything goes up (Your call)

Whatever is live that fortnight: a hire, a price, a client, a plan you keep putting off. All of it, so nothing stays in your head.

### We work through each one (Questions first)

I ask questions first, so the thinking on the whiteboard is yours. Then I give you my honest view.

### You leave with actions and dates (Every session)

Several decisions made, each with an action you can take before we next meet and a date against it.

### Next time, we check every one (Nothing slips)

What is done gets ticked. What no longer matters gets closed. The focus stays on what will move the business.

Online, with the whiteboard shared live on screen. If you are local to Milton Keynes, we can meet in person instead.

## What owners ask before they start

### Is this only for men?

No. The one-to-one is for any business owner. The circles are men only, and that is the only part of at.theboard that is.

### What if I do not know what to bring?

That is common. The first session starts with where the business is heading and what is in the way, and the decisions show up quickly from there.

### Is this consulting?

Not in the usual sense. I do not take your problem away and come back with a report. We think your challenges through together at the whiteboard, and I make suggestions drawn from my experience of working with small and large businesses. The decision is always yours.

### How is this different from a business coach I have tried before?

Every session ends with several decisions, each with an action and a date, and every one is checked next time. The price and end date are fixed from day one, and you can stop halfway if it is not working.

### How much does it cost?

£600 a month for six months, £3,600 in total, or £1,800 if you stop at three months.

### Is it online or in person?

Online by default, with the whiteboard shared live on screen. Owners local to Milton Keynes can meet in person.

### Can I move into a circle afterwards?

Yes, if you are a man and a place suits you. Men who apply have a session at the whiteboard first and then join the waiting list for the right circle.

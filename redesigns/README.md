# Five designs, one site

Five complete recreations of catalystgrowthcoach.co.uk. The words, the
photographs, the films, the programmes and the booking link are the same on
all five. The design is what changes. Each one is a genuinely different
concept, not a re-skin.

## The five concepts

**1. The Broadsheet** (catalyst-01-broadsheet.zip)
One line: a quality Sunday paper's long read, the practice presented as
serious journalism.
What makes it different: cream paper and ink, a proper masthead, columns
with hairline rules, drop caps, photographs run as documentary plates with
italic captions. Almost nothing moves. The confidence is in the typography.

**2. The Architect's Studio** (catalyst-02-architects-studio.zip)
One line: a modernist architect's portfolio, the practice presented as
built work.
What makes it different: white space, a visible drawing-board grid,
numbered plates, typewriter-style annotations, one drafting-blue accent.
Sessions read as projects; the footer is a draughtsman's title block.

**3. The Quiet Gallery** (catalyst-03-quiet-gallery.zip)
One line: a private gallery hang, each idea given a wall of its own.
What makes it different: the most spacious of the five. Every page is a
room, every photograph is hung and captioned like a work in a museum, and
there is no accent colour at all. It moves at gallery pace, with slow fades.

**4. The Title Sequence** (catalyst-04-title-sequence.zip)
One line: a film's opening titles, dark, cinematic, scroll-driven.
What makes it different: near-black throughout, your session films shown
full width like scenes, headings that arrive like title cards as you
scroll, a menu that opens like end credits. The darkest and most
atmospheric of the five.

**5. The Whiteboard** (catalyst-05-whiteboard.zip)
One line: one continuous thinking session, the whole site is a single
board worked from the first word to the solution.
What makes it different: it is one long page instead of separate pages.
It opens with "Stuck." being struck through in marker, and blue marker
strokes draw themselves in as you read. The signature line is the
structure of the site itself: thinking, board, solution.

## How to publish each one (step by step)

You do not need any technical knowledge for this. Each zip file is a
complete website. Netlify will host it for free.

1. In your web browser, go to **https://app.netlify.com/drop**
2. If Netlify asks you to sign up or log in, do that first (a free
   account is fine). You should end up on a page that says
   "Drag and drop your site output folder here".
3. Find one of the five zip files you downloaded, for example
   catalyst-01-broadsheet.zip. Do not open or unzip it.
4. Using your mouse, drag that zip file from your Downloads folder onto
   the drop area on the Netlify page, and let go.
5. Wait a few seconds. Netlify will unpack it and show you a link that
   looks something like https://wonderful-name-123456.netlify.app
6. Click the link. That is the site, live on the internet. You can send
   the link to anyone.
7. Repeat steps 3 to 6 for the other four zips, one at a time. Each one
   gets its own link.

Two things worth knowing:
- The links Netlify gives you have random names. You can change a name in
  Netlify under Site configuration, then "Change site name", to something
  like catalyst-broadsheet.netlify.app.
- These are demonstration copies. They do not touch your real website in
  any way, and nothing changes at catalystgrowthcoach.co.uk.

## Small print (differences from the live site, all deliberate)

- The booking button on every site goes to your current Dubsado scheduler,
  the same one the live site uses.
- The contact form looks and behaves like the live one (it shows your
  "I'll be in touch within one working day" confirmation). Like the live
  site's form, it does not yet send anywhere; wiring it up is a separate,
  small job on whichever design you choose.
- Google Analytics is left out of the demos on purpose, so demo visits do
  not pollute your real site statistics.
- On the In Practice page, Oli's card links to your LinkedIn profile,
  because the live site has an empty placeholder where his post link
  should be.
- Every em dash on the live site has been rewritten with commas or split
  sentences, in line with the voice rules.

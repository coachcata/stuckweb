# “Everyone around the table is capable. We still do not work as one.”

For leadership teams of five to ten who share the decisions, the pressure and the outcomes. I find out how your team really works, through the lens that suits you best. Then we work through what it shows you, at the board, until it changes how you lead together.

## You know this team is better than it performs

> “We agree in the meeting, and by Thursday nothing has moved.”

> “I know who is strongest at what. I am not sure they do.”

> “We are polite with each other, and I think the politeness is costing us.”

> “Some days I am sure it is that person over there. Other days I wonder if it is me.”

## There is more than one way to see how a team really works

Ask a leadership team how they work together and you will hear how they would like to work. To see how they actually work, you need to look from the outside, through more than one lens, and I choose the one that fits your team. Whichever we use, every session looks at both sides: how the team works together, and how its leader shows up.

## One day your team will still be talking about months later

A single, high impact day at your venue or ours, in the setting you choose. Each leads to the same place: a clear, shared picture of how you really work as a team, and two or three changes you agree before you leave.

### The site.

A working miniature construction site, six metres by three, brought to your venue. Your team operates the machines to complete a set task together. Within minutes the patterns show themselves: who communicates, who plans, who is strongest at which job, and what happens when the plan meets the ground.

### The board.

Your team brings a live problem it has not been able to settle, and we work it through together at the whiteboard until it is resolved. How you reach the answer tells you as much as the answer itself.

### The strengths lens.

Everyone completes FITT16 and CliftonStrengths beforehand. On the day the whole team’s profile goes on the board, so each person sees why they work the way they do, and why the person next to them works differently.

## When the change has to last

One high impact session a month, each in a different setting, so the team keeps moving. The mix is chosen for your team. A typical shape:

- Month one, the site.The team as it is today.
- Month two, the strengths lens.Why it works that way.
- Months three to five, the board.Real decisions, worked through together, with actions and dates checked each month.
- Month six, back to the site.The same task, the same team, and a very different result.

The leader has their own one-to-one sessions alongside. Price agreed with each team.

## Questions

### What space does the site need?

Two areas side by side: one for the construction site, which is six metres by three, and one for horseshoe seating where we talk. It works indoors or outdoors, and the machines are self-powered, so no power is needed.

### What if someone would rather not drive the machines?

That is fine. There is plenty to do without driving, and planning the task is often where the most revealing conversations happen.

### What is included in the price?

Everything. The Day is one price for the whole package, site included, from £3,500 plus VAT depending on numbers. For the Six Months, we agree one price before we start, and it includes the one-to-one sessions.

### Do we have to use the construction site?

No. The site is one of three settings, alongside the board and the strengths lens. We choose together what suits your team.

Four men as serious about their business as you are about yours

Five men who own and run businesses, meeting twice a month with a whiteboard and me, to work through whatever each of them is actually stuck on.

Whatever the weather

A business does not stop for a bad quarter

Your company goes through seasons. Good months, thin months, the quarter where three things go wrong at once. You do not get to sit those out, and neither does this.

So we walk in the rain. We walk in January. If it is coming down hard we take the shorter loop and get wetter, and the session happens exactly as it would have done.

You do not miss a meeting. That is not strictness for its own sake. It is the only way four other men can rely on you being there.

The method

What happens at the whiteboard

You start talking about the challenge, however it comes out. As you talk I write it up on the whiteboard, so the things that matter are out of your head and where you can see them. Then the real work starts.

The question nobody asks out loud

“Will I be the one who is behind?”

Almost everybody wonders it. Almost nobody says it.

Circles are not built by size of business. They are built by whether five men are stuck on challenges they can usefully think about together. Nine staff and a partnership falling apart is more use to twenty-four staff and no successor than any matching turnover would be.

You will be ahead of the others on some things and behind on others. That is the arrangement, and it is the only way to find out where you actually are.

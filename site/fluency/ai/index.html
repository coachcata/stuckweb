The Fluency Series

AI

Fluent, not fluent in hype. The three levels, free to take.

The barrier was never intelligence — it’s that AI arrived faster than the language to think about it clearly. So leaders nod along to the vendor, or freeze, when the job is to judge. These three short guides give you the words, the real story behind the promises, and the questions that keep AI accountable to you — rather than the other way round. Read them in order; each one gives the next something to grip.

The three levels
Part One
The Language

Model, token, hallucination, agent — the words behind the hype, in plain English. Learn the vocabulary and the vendor demo stops being a spell and starts being something you can weigh.

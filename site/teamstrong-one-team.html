How it works

There is more than one way to see how a team really works

Ask a leadership team how they work together and you will hear how they would like to work. To see how they actually work, you need to look from the outside, through more than one lens, and I choose the one that fits your team. Whichever we use, every session looks at both sides: how the team works together, and how its leader shows up.

One Day Programme

One day your team will still be talking about months later

A single, high impact day at your venue or ours at the Shire Retreat, Milton Keynes, in the setting you choose. Each leads to the same place: a clear, shared picture of how you really work as a team, and two or three changes you agree before you leave.

Setting one

The immersive building experience.

A team building experience that everyone takes part in. On a mini construction site with real small diggers and machines, six metres by three and brought to your venue, your team plans and builds together through competitions and activities. Along the way we talk about how you communicate, how you plan, who takes ownership and how you work with each other.

Site footage coming soon.

Setting two

The board.

We harness the creativity and problem solving of the whole group, live at the whiteboard. It gives your team the chance to nail down solutions to problems that have been running for a while, and I guide you to set out plans to put those solutions into action and test them.

Setting three

The strengths lens.

When cohesion and collaboration are the focus, you need to go beyond skin deep. Everyone completes FITT16 and CliftonStrengths beforehand, so team members understand each other at a deep level, work more productively together and head off conflict before it starts.

From £3,500 plus VAT, depending on the size of your team. One price for your team, agreed up front, with everything included.

Six Month Programme

When we want lasting change

One high impact session a month, each in a different setting, so the team keeps moving. The mix is chosen for your team. A typical shape:

  1. Month one, the immersive building experience.

    The team as it is today.

  2. Month two, the strengths lens.

    Why it works that way.

  3. Months three to five, the board.

    Real decisions, worked through together, with actions and dates checked each month.

  4. Month six, back to the immersive building experience.

    The same task, the same team, and a very different result.

The leader has their own one-to-one sessions alongside. Price agreed with each team.

Questions

Questions

What space does the immersive building experience need?

Two areas side by side: one for the immersive building experience, which is six metres by three, and one for horseshoe seating where we talk. It works indoors or outdoors, and the machines are self-powered, so no power is needed.

What if someone would rather not drive the machines?

That is fine. There is plenty to do without driving, and planning the task is often where the most revealing conversations happen.

What is included in the price?

Everything. The One Day Programme is one price for the whole package, immersive building experience included, from £3,500 plus VAT depending on numbers. For the Six Month Programme, we agree one price before we start, and it includes the one-to-one sessions.

Do we have to do the immersive building experience?

No. The immersive building experience is one of three settings, alongside the board and the strengths lens. We choose together what suits your team.
